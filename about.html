OUR STORY

Born From the Community. Built to Support Local Business.

I grew up here in Oxnard, and I've seen firsthand how important small businesses are to our community.

Restaurants, contractors, bakeries, shops, laundromats, auto businesses, professional offices, and family-owned companies are part of what keeps our community moving.

Oxnard IT Solutions was created to provide those businesses with a local technology resource they can turn to when they need a website, better networking, technology setup, server infrastructure, or simply help solving an IT problem.

The goal isn't to make technology more complicated. It's to understand the problem, explain the options clearly, and build a solution that makes sense for the business.
