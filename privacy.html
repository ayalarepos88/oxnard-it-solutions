PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

How Oxnard IT Solutions handles information submitted through our website and used in connection with our services.

Effective Date: October 28, 2026
Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Oxnard IT Solutions respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit oxnarditsolutions.com, contact us, or use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when contacting Oxnard IT Solutions, including:

  • Your name
  • Business or organization name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business type
  • Service or project of interest
  • Preferred contact method
  • Information you choose to include in a contact form, consultation request, or message
  • Information reasonably necessary to understand or provide requested IT services

You are not required to provide information through our online forms unless you choose to submit a request.

Please do not submit sensitive credentials.

Our website forms are not intended for passwords, Social Security numbers, payment-card information, authentication codes, private keys, recovery codes, or other highly sensitive credentials.

How We Use Information

Information collected by Oxnard IT Solutions may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and service inquiries
  • Communicate with prospective and existing customers
  • Discuss requested IT services
  • Prepare quotes, estimates, invoices, and project proposals
  • Schedule and provide requested IT services
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain records related to services provided
  • Protect our website, forms, customers, and systems from spam, fraud, abuse, and malicious activity
  • Maintain and improve the security and operation of our website and services

We do not use information submitted through our contact forms for unrelated purposes without appropriate notice or permission.

Website & Security Information

Oxnard IT Solutions uses third-party infrastructure and security services, including Cloudflare, to help deliver and protect our website.

Cloudflare Turnstile is used with website forms to help detect automated traffic, spam, and abuse.

Our website, hosting infrastructure, and security systems may process technical information associated with website requests, such as:

  • IP address
  • Request timestamps
  • Requested pages or resources
  • Browser or user-agent information
  • Device or connection information
  • Security-related information or events

This information may be used for website operation, troubleshooting, security, rate limiting, fraud prevention, and abuse prevention.

Email Communications

Contact and consultation requests submitted through the website may be delivered to an Oxnard IT Solutions email account so the request can be reviewed and answered.

If you provide an email address, Oxnard IT Solutions may use that address to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you regarding requested or existing services.

Quotes, Invoices & Payments

Oxnard IT Solutions may use third-party payment and invoicing providers, including Stripe, to provide quotes, invoices, payment pages, receipts, and related payment services.

Payment information submitted directly through a third-party payment provider is processed according to that provider's applicable privacy practices and terms.

Oxnard IT Solutions may receive transaction information necessary for invoicing, payment status, business records, customer support, accounting, and related administrative purposes.

Service Providers

Oxnard IT Solutions may use third-party service providers to operate and support our website and business.

These services may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure
  • Domain name and DNS services
  • Website security and bot protection
  • Email and communications services
  • Payment processing and invoicing
  • Cloud and technology services

These providers may process information as reasonably necessary to provide their respective services.

Selling Personal Information

Oxnard IT Solutions does not sell personal information submitted through our website contact or consultation forms.

Sharing of Information

Oxnard IT Solutions may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide services requested by a customer
  • Work with service providers that support our business operations
  • Protect our systems, customers, or business against fraud, abuse, or security threats
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or lawful requests

Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

This may include responding to inquiries, providing requested services, maintaining business and transaction records, resolving disputes, maintaining security, and meeting applicable legal, contractual, accounting, or administrative obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

Your Privacy Choices

You may choose not to submit an online consultation or contact form and may use another available contact method instead.

You may also contact Oxnard IT Solutions regarding personal information you previously provided, including requests concerning access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Certain information may need to be retained when reasonably necessary for legitimate business, security, accounting, contractual, or legal purposes.

California Privacy

California residents may have additional privacy rights under applicable California privacy laws.

Depending on the law and whether it applies to Oxnard IT Solutions, these rights may include certain rights concerning access, correction, or deletion of personal information.

Privacy-related requests may be submitted using the contact information provided at the end of this policy.

Browser Privacy Signals

Some browsers and devices provide privacy, tracking-preference, or similar signals.

Oxnard IT Solutions does not currently use information submitted through our contact forms for cross-site behavioral advertising.

Our treatment of browser privacy signals may change as our website, technologies, services, and applicable legal requirements change.

Children's Privacy

Oxnard IT Solutions provides business and technology services. Our website and services are not intentionally designed or directed toward children.

If you believe information relating to a child has been provided to us inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Security

Oxnard IT Solutions uses reasonable technical and administrative measures designed to protect information under our control.

However, no website, internet transmission, electronic-storage method, or security system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services & Links

Our website may use or link to independent third-party websites, platforms, or services.

Third-party services operate according to their own terms, privacy policies, and information-handling practices.

We encourage users to review the applicable policies of third-party services they choose to use.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Oxnard IT Solutions may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, technology providers, or information-handling practices change.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the Last Updated date displayed at the top of this page will be revised.
